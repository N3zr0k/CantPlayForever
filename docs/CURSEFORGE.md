# CurseForge

## Summary
Clock with alarms, timers, playtime and Health reminders for WoW Forever.

## Description
**Can't play Forever** replaces the minimap clock with a configurable clock that carries multiple alarms and timers.

* **Alarms**: daily, on chosen weekdays or on a date (monthly or yearly), once or repeating; types alarm clock, event and reminder. Ctrl + left-click arms / disarms.
* **Timers**: several presets, start and stop with one click.
* **Playtime**: tooltip with current and last session; hold Shift for `/played` of every known character and the account total.
* **Health** (off by default): reminders to move, drink and rest your eyes, with your own texts if you like.
* **Effects**: sound, chat message, on-screen message and an adjustable glow at the screen edge and on the clock, per module.
* **Original clock**: can be hidden; Ctrl + right-click opens the stopwatch, Alt + right-click the original clock menu.
* English, German and French.
