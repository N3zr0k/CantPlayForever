# CurseForge

## Summary
Clock with alarms, timers, playtime, holiday calendar and Health reminders for WoW Forever.

## Description
**Can't play Forever** replaces the minimap clock with a configurable clock that carries multiple alarms and timers.

* **Alarms**: daily, on chosen weekdays or on a date (monthly or yearly), once or repeating; types alarm clock, event and reminder. Ctrl + left-click arms / disarms.
* **Timers**: several presets, start and stop with one click.
* **Playtime**: tooltip with current and last session; hold Shift for `/played` of every known character and the account total.
* **Holiday calendar**: the clock shows the icon of a running holiday with a themed decoration; the tooltips list the running and the next holiday with a countdown. Times follow your clock setting (local or server time).
* **Alarms in the calendar**: your alarms appear in the in-game calendar, only on your computer and never as a server event, or in *Classic Calendar - Revived* if installed. A click opens the alarm in the options.
* **Calendar events as alarms**: on request, never automatically, turn your calendar events (in-game calendar or Classic Calendar - Revived) into alarms. Changed events update their alarm, alarms of deleted events can be removed.
* **Details on demand**: hold Ctrl over the clock for alarm details and the next calendar events with creator, response, attendees and description.
* **Health** (off by default): reminders to move, drink and rest your eyes, with your own texts if you like.
* **Effects**: sound, chat message, on-screen message and an adjustable glow at the screen edge and on the clock, per module.
* **Original clock**: can be hidden; Ctrl + right-click opens the stopwatch, Alt + right-click the original clock menu.
* English, German and French.
